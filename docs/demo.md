# Student OS competition demo

## Objective

Show that Student OS turns Cornell's scattered resources into a connected ecosystem students can explore.

## Exact demo path

1. Open Student OS. Cornell is the configured university for this prototype.
2. In onboarding, select `AI / Technology` and `Entrepreneurship`; optionally select `Johnson School of Management`, `MBA`, and `Year 1`.
3. Show the personalized home as `My Cornell OS`.
4. Open `Discover` and open `Big Red AI` or `Cornell AppDev`.
5. Open `Explore → Entrepreneurship` and briefly show eLab, Blackstone LaunchPad, and Cornell Entrepreneur Network.
6. Ask: `I'm interested in AI entrepreneurship. What should I explore?`
7. Show the Connected Journey and its next action.
8. Open a related resource such as CUeLINKS, Cornell StartupTree, or eLab.
9. Return Home and point out Recently explored / personalized discovery.

## Expected Journey

The exact ranking can vary with local history, but the Journey must use existing Cornell entities and relationships. Expected cross-category examples include:

- Big Red AI or Generative AI at Cornell
- Cornell StartupTree or another people/network resource
- Entrepreneurship at Cornell, eLab, or Blackstone LaunchPad
- A related community, event, or opportunity
- An explicitly labeled student-built discovery when available

The app must not claim that a person, date, deadline, eligibility rule, or relationship exists unless it is present in the dataset.

## Fallback

If the Journey cannot be generated, use the grouped Ask results. Do not invent a path. If an external resource is unavailable, keep the entity visible and use its related entities or source page.

## Demo copy

> Cornell has more to offer than students know. Student OS helps students discover what exists, what connects to what, and where to go next.

## External source fallback

The app links to the original source URL and does not proxy or mirror external content. If a source is unavailable, explain that clearly and continue with the verified entity description and connected resources.
